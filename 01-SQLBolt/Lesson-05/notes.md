# Lesson 4

## Concepts Learned

- Practiced **SIMPLE QUERIES** from previous concepts.
- Used **WHERE , ORDER BY and LIMIT** all together at once.
- Used queries at **real data** and **problems**.
- **OFFSET X** is used to skip the top X records only.

## Common Mistakes

- Order of **execution of queries** is wrong.
- Not reading **problem statements** carefully.

## Key Takeaways

- Take care of **order of execution**.
- Read **problem statements** carefully.

## Important Query Structure

A common **query structure** is:

SELECT column_name  
FROM table_name  
WHERE condition(s)  
ORDER BY column_name DESC/ASC  
LIMIT X OFFSET Y ;
