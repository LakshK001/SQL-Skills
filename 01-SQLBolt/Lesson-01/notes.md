# Lesson 1

## Concepts Learned

- **SELECT** is used to retrieve data from table.
- **SELECT** specific columns from table.
- **SELECT** all columns from table.

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting **comma** between columns.
- Forgetting the **semi colon** at the end of the query.

## Key Takeaways

- **Avoid** SELECT * unless needed.
- **SQL** is case insensitive.
